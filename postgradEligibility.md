# Graduate Degree (MS) Programs

## MS Civil Engineering
**Specializations:** Structure, Geotechnical, Environmental, Construction Engineering Management

### Eligibility Criteria
1. Minimum 16-year education in relevant field with minimum 2.0 CGPA or 60% marks from University / DAI recognized by HEC and accredited by relevant accreditation body (PEC).
2. Qualifying GAT-General Test of CECOS University or any other approved testing body (NTS/ETEA).

# Ph.D. Programs

## Ph.D. Civil Engineering

### Eligibility Criteria
1. Minimum 18-year Master Degree (Research Based) in relevant field with minimum 3.0 CGPA from HEC recognized University.
2. Candidates who completed Master degree through coursework will be required to publish one research paper in an HEC recognized journal prior to admission.
